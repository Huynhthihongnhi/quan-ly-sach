## Questions counsel must resolve

1. **Ownership and earlier grants.** Confirm which original additions the licensor can license. Review contributor/employer permissions, mixed files, the 26 MIT skills, Mermaid notices and any earlier MIT copies. Include the [explanation skill provenance review](SKILL_PROVENANCE_REVIEW.md): the identified upstream is Matt Pocock, the exact historical import remains unknown, and 0.6.3 supplements the MIT notices. Assess prior distributions and the new name as appropriate. Do not treat a new package name or license header as proof of exclusive ownership.
2. **Seats, clients and AI services.** Review one-user use on employer devices, client output, generated instruction blocks, private modifications, agent-provider processing, team sharing and redistribution. Confirm that customers can understand which files may enter a public application repository.
3. **Seller and buyer markets.** Determine applicable seller disclosures, mandatory buyer protections, contract language, governing law, dispute process and appropriate venue. Worldwide buyer intent is recorded; no governing-law clause, cleared market list or consumer/business classification has been selected.
4. **Checkout and acceptance.** Specify how the complete license and order terms are shown before payment or gift acceptance, which action records agreement, and how buyers retain their accepted terms. Define evidence retention and corrections without collecting unnecessary personal data.
5. **Refunds, cancellation and renewals.** Draft the actual policy for one-time purchases and each recurring offer. Review any mandatory withdrawal rights for digital content, renewal notice or consent requirements, failed payments, partial refunds and disputes in the intended markets.
6. **Delivered copies and updates.** Reconcile continued use of delivered versions, an explicit fixed use term, update expiry, subscription cancellation, refund consequences and termination. Confirm the 30-day cure provision and mandatory exceptions. The kit cannot revoke already distributed MIT rights or recall downloaded files technically.
7. **Warranty and liability.** Review section 7 for the seller and buyer jurisdictions. Decide whether the language needs changes, including any limitation amount, mandatory carve-outs and enforceability conditions. No enforceability conclusion is assumed here.
8. **Payment, privacy and tax.** Review the chosen provider's current terms and allocation of responsibilities. Identify required privacy disclosures, customer-data handling, retention, international transfers and Vietnam business/accounting obligations with appropriate advisers. Provider onboarding does not establish legal clearance.
